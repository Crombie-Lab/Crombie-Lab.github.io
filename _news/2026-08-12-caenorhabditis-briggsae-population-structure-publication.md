Our collaborative paper, [“Population structure and punctuated genomic hyper-diversity in _Caenorhabditis briggsae_”](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msag202), is published in _Molecular Biology and Evolution_. Large-scale wild-strain genomics reveals population structure and highly divergent genomic regions.
