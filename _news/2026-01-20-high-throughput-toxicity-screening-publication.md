Our review, [“High-Throughput Toxicity Screening with _C. elegans_: Current Platforms, Key Advantages, and Future Directions”](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.5c12562), appears in _Environmental Science & Technology_. We evaluate automated nematode toxicity assays and opportunities to expand their use in chemical screening.
