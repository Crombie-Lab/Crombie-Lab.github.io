Our collaborative paper, [“Global genomic diversity of the selfing nematode _Caenorhabditis tropicalis_ correlates with geography”](https://doi.org/10.1093/genetics/iyag196), is published in _GENETICS_. Genome-wide variation reveals geographic population structure and concentrated regions of extreme sequence diversity.
