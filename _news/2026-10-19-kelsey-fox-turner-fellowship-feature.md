**Kelsey Fox** was featured in [Florida Tech News](https://news.fit.edu/academics-research/two-florida-tech-students-awarded-turner-endowed-fellowships/) as a recipient of the **Dr. Richard Lincoln Turner Endowed Research Fund fellowship** ($1,658). The award supports her research investigating how natural variation in cytochrome P450 metabolism contributes to chlorfenapyr resistance in *Caenorhabditis elegans*. Congratulations, Kelsey!
