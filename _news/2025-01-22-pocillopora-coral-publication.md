Our collaborative paper, [“Geographical patterns of intraspecific genetic diversity reflect the adaptive potential of the coral _Pocillopora damicornis_ species complex”](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0316380), is published in _PLOS ONE_. The study examines geographic variation in coral genetic diversity and its potential relevance to climate resilience.
