Our paper, [“Natural variation suggests candidate genes underlying _Caenorhabditis elegans_ susceptibility to diverse toxicants”](https://doi.org/10.1093/toxsci/kfag019), appears in _Toxicological Sciences_. Screening 195 wild strains against 23 toxicants identified genomic regions and candidate genes associated with susceptibility differences.
