Our paper, [“CaeNDR, the _Caenorhabditis_ Natural Diversity Resource”](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkad887), appears in the January 2024 issue of _Nucleic Acids Research_. The expanded resource supports natural-variation and genetic-mapping studies across three _Caenorhabditis_ species.
